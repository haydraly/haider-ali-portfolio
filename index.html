about me

Turning Digital Noise Into Profitable Pipelines.

Bridging the gap between user attention spans and business conversion loops through analytical campaign testing and full funnel asset design.
I still remember walking away from psychology internships because I realized my true obsession was not just studying human emotion but to apply that where it matters most, which is understanding exactly what makes someone stop scrolling, click & make a buying decision in a split second. The two worlds are actually deeply connected because the same psychological principles used to analyze behavior are the hidden keys to mastering modern advertising algorithms.
My mission is to build profitable acquisition systems that completely eliminate guesswork and back every single ad dollar with tracking precision. Whether I am orchestrating organic cross platform narratives as a Social Media Lead or engineering high intent performance marketing funnels, I focus entirely on metrics that scale business profit. By combining thumb-stopping creative strategy with bulletproof technical tracking, I help brands stop wasting media spend and start dominating modern feeds using highly targeted paid media scaling.
Over the past 4.5+ years, I have managed hundreds of thousands of dollars in ad spend across highly volatile niches. My portfolio includes optimizing a CAD $45,000/month Google Ads infrastructure alongside a USD $25,000/month Meta Ads campaign for the North American mortgage market, scaling DTC e-commerce brands to a 5x ROAS via Pinterest Ads with a €47,000 monthly spend, and architecting high-converting real estate lead generation engines in the UAE. Additionally, I have engineered social media strategies and dine-in campaigns for prominent Dubai F&B brands. My approach centers on deploying full-funnel content pipelines, advanced GTM and GA4 tracking attribution, and high-ROI PPC campaigns that transform vanity metrics into dependable revenue.
